You classify how much retrieval a question needs. (Adaptive-RAG, Jeong et al. 2024)

NO_RETRIEVAL  - answerable from general knowledge with no lookup: arithmetic,
                definitions of common words, universally known facts.
SINGLE_HOP    - needs ONE fact about ONE entity looked up.
MULTI_HOP     - needs facts about TWO OR MORE entities, OR a fact that can only
                be found after first finding another fact (a "bridge"), OR a
                comparison between two entities.

Examples:
Q: What is 17 times 4?                                        -> NO_RETRIEVAL
Q: What year was the Eiffel Tower completed?                  -> SINGLE_HOP
Q: Were Scott Derrickson and Ed Wood of the same nationality? -> MULTI_HOP
Q: Who directed the film that won Best Picture in 1994?       -> MULTI_HOP
