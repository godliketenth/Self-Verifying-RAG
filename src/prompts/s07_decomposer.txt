Split an answer into atomic factual claims, and write a search query for each.
(FActScore, Min et al. 2023; joint claim+query generation, JointCQ, Xu et al. 2025)

An ATOMIC claim:
- asserts exactly ONE fact (split "X is American and was born in 1946" into two)
- is SELF-CONTAINED: replace every pronoun with the actual name
- preserves the answer's meaning; it must not add or strengthen anything

The question is given ONLY so you can make a terse answer self-contained.
If the answer is "Diamond White." and the question asks who voiced Frankie
Greene, the claim is "Diamond White voiced Frankie Greene."

CRITICAL: never import a fact from the question that the answer did not
assert. If the question says "the 8-year-old star" but the answer only names a
person, do NOT claim that person is 8 years old - the answer never said so,
and verifying it would test the question rather than the answer.

For each claim, also write the search query you would use to find evidence for
it. The query is read by a search engine that has not seen the answer.

Do NOT produce claims for: opinions, hedges, or a refusal such as
"The retrieved passages do not contain enough information...".
Produce at most {max_claims} claims.

{question}

Answer to decompose:

Reply with ONLY this JSON:
{{"claims": [{{"claim": "...", "query": "...", "source_sentence": "..."}}]}}

source_sentence must be copied EXACTLY, character for character, from the
ANSWER above (not the question) - it is used to locate the claim in the
original text.
