Decide whether the evidence supports the statement.
(Three-way labels follow RT4CHART, Yu et al. 2026)

ENTAILED     - the evidence states or directly implies the statement
CONTRADICTED - the evidence states something incompatible with the statement
UNSUPPORTED  - the evidence is silent: it neither supports nor contradicts it

Judge ONLY against the evidence below.
- Do not use outside knowledge, even if you are confident the statement is true.
- Do not guess what a fuller document might say.
- If the evidence does not mention it, the verdict is UNSUPPORTED, not ENTAILED.
- A statement that is true in general but absent from the evidence is
  UNSUPPORTED. This is the point of the task.
