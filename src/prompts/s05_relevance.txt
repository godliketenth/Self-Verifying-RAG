Judge how much each passage helps answer the question. (CRAG, Yan et al. 2024)

IMPORTANT: many questions are MULTI-HOP - answering them needs facts from two
or more different passages. A passage that supplies only ONE of the needed
facts is still fully relevant. Do not penalise a passage for being incomplete
on its own.

Score each passage from 0.0 to 1.0:
  1.0  supplies a fact needed for ANY PART of the answer, including one hop
       of a multi-hop question, or identifies an entity the question names
  0.5  about a named entity in the question, but supplies no needed fact
  0.0  unrelated, or shares only surface words with the question

Example: for "Were Scott Derrickson and Ed Wood of the same nationality?", a
passage giving ONLY Scott Derrickson's nationality scores 1.0 - it answers one
hop. It is not 0.5 just because it says nothing about Ed Wood.

Be strict only about genuine irrelevance: a passage about a different person
with a similar name scores 0.0.
